# Parcel ID not linked to SBI in SITI Tenure (SFI23)

## Introduction

This guide is designed to explain the process on how to check "SFI Parcel ID not linked to SBI in SITI Tenure" cases.

The SFI Parcel ID is checked in SITI Tenure to ensure the land parcel on the agreement is still linked to the Agreement Holder. The case raises for each parcel that fails the check.

## Parcel no longer linked to SBI investigation.

For any land parcel to have been included in the SFI agreement at the point of setting live, it would have been linked to the SBI in LPIS (Land Parcel Identification System).

If the system check has identified an error which indicates that the land parcel(s) listed in the case is no longer registered to the SBI, you must investigate why and determine whether the parcel can remain in the SFI agreement.

It could be that the land parcel has transferred to another SBI or otherwise become unlinked (for example, because of an expiring parcel link), or mapping changes may have caused the land parcel reference to no longer be linked to the SBI due to the parcel splitting or merging with another parcel. This could be for reasons including:

- Customer request (mapping updates instigated by the Land Manager)
- PLCD (Proactive Land Change Detection) - mapping updates made by RPA because of:
  - OS (Ordnance Survey) data
  - Aerial photography
  - Satellite imagery
- Site visit/Inspection findings

Note: A case may raise for a parcel which requires investigation for being both no longer on LPIS and no longer linked to SBI.' In this scenario, make a note of any land parcels which have both cases present. Work the 'Parcel no longer on LPIS' case first, and once successfully resolved, any parcels which are also detailed within the 'Parcel no longer linked to SBI' case do not need to be investigated; this case can then be closed following the entry of a case note which references the Case ID and corresponding land parcel numbers of the 'SFI PARCEL ID NOT FOUND ON THE LPIS' case.

### Parcel Tenure checks

- Yes, the parcel is linked to the SBI, and the task has raised in error. Refer to Case resolution and closure guidance.

### Mapping checks

You must investigate whether mapping changes have caused the land parcel which you are investigating to become unlinked from the SBI.

Navigate to the Land Tab in SITI Farmer, following SFI SITI Agri Basic Navigation section 'SITI Farmer' if you need guidance on how to do so, and for general reference in undertaking the checks listed in this guide.

Locate the land parcel which you are investigating, using SFI SITI Agri Basic Navigation  section 'SITI Farmer' for guidance. You must select back through previous years to identify the parcel.

Once the land parcel is present in the map, make a note of its size and relationship with the surrounding parcels (for example, '1.25ha, located north of parcel XX1111 2345'), and then compare the changes in mapping by moving back forwards through mapping years towards the scheme year you are currently working.

#### Split/Merge

A split is defined as a land parcel splitting into two or more parcels; a merge is defined as two or more land parcels merging to create less than the original number of parcels involved.

In either of these scenarios, it is possible that the land parcel which is part of the SFI agreement is no longer present in SITI Farmer as a result of splitting into, or merging with, other parcels - the location will still be mapped, but the original parcel reference no longer exists and the area of land previously covered by the parcel which you are investigating will be covered by another parcel or parcels.

### Check CRM

You must now check CRM for any correspondence relating to the land parcel which you are investigating, which may assist in your investigation into why the parcel is no longer linked to the SBI.

Navigate to CRM and search for the SBI you are investigating, using CRM Basic Navigation and Admin section 2.3 if you need guidance on how to do so.

Within the 'Activities' tab for the SBI or within any related CRM cases, search for any related correspondence which may help in your investigation, in particular any sold parcels, land transfer (for instance, where you can identify that a transfer involving the parcel(s) concerned has been requested and explains the link to any other business which you noted at section 5.1.2 of this guidance), or mapping requests.

This could include:

- Email/letters.
- RLE1 forms
- Rural Land Changes
- Maps

You must also perform a check of CRM for any other SBIs which you have identified as being linked to the parcel.

Have you identified any evidence to confirm that the land parcel which you are investigating must no longer be linked to the SBI?

- Yes, the parcel must be removed from the agreement. Make a note of the reason for the parcel being unlinked from the SBI and refer to the make a note of the reason for the parcel being unlinked from the SBI and refer to the 'Learner check' section.
- No, are you able to confirm that an error has delinked the parcel from the SBI?
  - Yes, you can confirm that an error has delinked the parcel from the SBI, a referral to GI Operations is required to request the parcel is relinked. Continue to section GI Operations referral.
  - No, must contact the agreement holder to query this. Make a note of the reason for the parcel being unlinked from the SBI and refer to the section: 'Learner check.'
